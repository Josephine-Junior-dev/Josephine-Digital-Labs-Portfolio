THE STORY BEHIND LIVING BUDGET

Borrowed Light.

Sometimes, you borrow not because you want more, but because what you have is not enough.

You borrow money for electricity, gas, transport, food, phone bundles, or other basic needs. You promise to repay tomorrow, hoping things will improve. But tomorrow comes with new expenses, and yesterday’s debts are still waiting.

You may have a salary, a small business, or a daily income, yet still struggle to understand where the money goes. Before you receive it, you have already planned how to spend it. Unexpected expenses appear, debts accumulate, and you find yourself borrowing again just to keep life moving.

The problem

How can someone manage money better when every shilling already has a responsibility?

Many people do not need complicated financial systems. They need a simple way to record income, track expenses, understand debts, plan their budgets, and see what remains before making another financial decision.

Borrowed Light represents the struggle of trying to keep life moving when your money is already stretched thin.

From the problem to Living Budget

Living Budget was inspired by the reality that managing money is not just about how much you earn. It is also about understanding where your money goes, what you owe, and what you can realistically afford.

The project explores how a simple digital tool could help people organize their finances, monitor expenses, and plan ahead. It is an early-stage project that I am continuing to learn from and improve.

Borrowed Light is more than a story about hardship. It is about recognizing a real problem and using personal experience to inspire a possible solution.

THE VISION

See your money clearly.
Choose your next step.

Help people move from borrowing to survive toward planning to thrive.
